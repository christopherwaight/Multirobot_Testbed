This appendix gives the forward kinematics, inverse kinematics, and
inverse kinematic Jacobian for the six-robot formation-control layer used
by Chapter~\ref{ch:second_order}, distinct from that chapter's estimator,
which reads only the six measured robot positions and does not depend on
this parameterization. The six robots are grouped into three rigid pairs,
and the pair midpoints form a triangle; this is the pentagon-\emph{of-pairs}
configuration, which generates the nominal pentagon-plus-center formation
only at the specific ring-radius setpoint used in that chapter's
simulations.

\section{Forward Kinematics}
\label{sec:appC:forward}

The pentagon-of-pairs cluster has six robots indexed $i=1,\ldots,6$,
arranged as three pairs. Pair $k$ ($k=1,2,3$) consists of robots $2k-1$
and $2k$. The midpoint of pair $k$ is
$\mathbf{m}_k = (\mathbf{p}_{2k-1}+\mathbf{p}_{2k})/2$. The three
midpoints form a triangle; the centroid of that triangle is
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{p}_c = \frac{\mathbf{m}_1+\mathbf{m}_2+\mathbf{m}_3}{3}.
\label{eq:appC:centroid}
\end{equation}
The SAS parameters of the midpoint triangle are defined as in the
three-robot case of Appendix~\ref{app:kin3}:
$p_1 = \lVert \mathbf{m}_2-\mathbf{m}_1 \rVert$,
$q_1 = \lVert \mathbf{m}_3-\mathbf{m}_2 \rVert$, and $\beta_1$ is the
interior angle at $\mathbf{m}_2$. The cluster heading is
\begin{equation}
\theta_c = \operatorname{atan2}(m_{2,y}-m_{1,y},\, m_{2,x}-m_{1,x}).
\label{eq:appC:theta_c}
\end{equation}
Each pair is described by its half-length $L_k/2$ and orientation angle
$\psi_k$, so the two robots in pair $k$ are at
$\mathbf{m}_k \pm (L_k/2)(\cos\psi_k,\sin\psi_k)^{\mathsf T}$.

The full twelve-state cluster configuration is
\begin{equation}
(x_c,y_c,\theta_c,\,p_1,\beta_1,q_1,\,L_2,\psi_2,\,L_3,\psi_3,\,L_4,\psi_4),
\label{eq:appC:state}
\end{equation}
where pair 1 uses $L_1$ fixed and $\psi_1 = \theta_c$ by convention.

\section{Inverse Kinematics}
\label{sec:appC:inverse}

Given the twelve-state cluster configuration, the six robot positions are
recovered by: constructing the midpoint triangle from $(p_1,\beta_1,q_1)$
in a local frame, following the same procedure as the three-robot
triangle of Appendix~\ref{app:kin3}; rotating and translating to the
global frame using $\theta_c$, $x_c$, $y_c$; and placing each pair of
robots by offsetting from its midpoint along $(\cos\psi_k,\sin\psi_k)$ by
$L_k/2$ in each direction.

\section{Inverse Kinematic Jacobian}
\label{sec:appC:jacobian}

The $12\times12$ inverse kinematic Jacobian maps desired cluster state
velocities $\dot{\boldsymbol{\xi}}$ to robot velocity commands
$(\dot x_1,\dot y_1,\ldots,\dot x_6,\dot y_6)^{\mathsf T}$:
\begin{equation}
\begin{bmatrix} \dot x_1\\\dot y_1\\\vdots\\\dot x_6\\\dot y_6 \end{bmatrix}
= \mathbf{J}_{c,\text{pentagon}}^{-1}
\begin{bmatrix} \dot x_c\\\dot y_c\\\dot\theta_c\\\dot p_1\\\dot\beta_1\\\dot q_1\\\dot L_2\\\dot\psi_2\\\dot L_3\\\dot\psi_3\\\dot L_4\\\dot\psi_4 \end{bmatrix},
\label{eq:appC:inv_kin_jac}
\end{equation}
where $\mathbf{J}_{c,\text{pentagon}}^{-1}$ is a $12\times12$ matrix of
partial derivatives $\partial(x_i,y_i)/\partial\xi_j$ computed
analytically from the inverse kinematics above.
The entries are not listed here. In the simulation software they are
assembled from analytic partial derivatives of the pair-midpoint
construction above (\texttt{compute\_inverse\_jacobian} in
\texttt{src/control/pentagon\_kinematics.py}), and the
\texttt{cluster\_builder} tool generates them in closed symbolic form
(\texttt{clusterbuilder.py} with the \texttt{--symbolic} option).
