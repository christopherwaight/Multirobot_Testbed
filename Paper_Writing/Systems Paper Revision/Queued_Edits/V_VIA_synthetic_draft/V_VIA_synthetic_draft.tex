\subsection{Validation Test Plan}

For each field, eight starting locations were selected around the perimeter of the workspace and the attraction controller (10) was run 10 times from each, using the three-robot equilateral formation of side \open{$s$}, for 80 trials per field. The orbit controller (11) was run at \VtxOrbThreeRadii{} commanded radii from 0.01 to 0.50~m, \VtxOrbThreeTrials{} times each, for \open{60}~s per run. Run order was randomized.

Each attraction trial $i$ is scored by its final error $e_i = \|\mathbf{p}_{c,i}(T) - \mathbf{p}^*\|$. A trial converges at threshold $\varepsilon$ if $e_i \le \varepsilon$ and the centroid never leaves the printed map. Otherwise it fails. Success is reported at $\varepsilon = 0.03$, $0.04$ and $0.05$~m. Convergence time $t_\varepsilon$ is the time after which the centroid stays within $\varepsilon$. Accuracy is reported two ways. The mean error $\bar e$ averages $e_i$, and the bias $b = \|\bar{\mathbf{p}}_c(T) - \mathbf{p}^*\|$ is the distance of the mean final position, with precision $\sigma$ the RMS distance of the final positions from that mean. Localization error $\bar e_{\hat p}$ is the mean of $\|\hat{\mathbf{p}}^* - \mathbf{p}^*\|$ over the last 20\% of a trial. Control effort is the centroid path length $L = \sum_j \|\mathbf{p}_c[j{+}1] - \mathbf{p}_c[j]\|$.

Each orbit run is scored on the radial error $e_r(t) = \|\mathbf{p}_c(t) - \mathbf{p}^*\| - r_d$ after the first 10~s, the same exclusion as Section~IV. A run gives its mean $\bar e_r$ and its within-run standard deviation. Across the repeats at one radius, the mean of $\bar e_r$ is the radial bias and its standard deviation is the between-trial spread. The controller-only error replaces $\mathbf{p}^*$ with $\hat{\mathbf{p}}^*$. A radius is practically achievable when $|\bar e_r| \le 0.25\,r_d$. An orbit fails if the centroid leaves the map.

\subsection{Orbital Control Results}

Table~\ref{tab:orbit} and Fig.~\ref{fig:orbit} give the orbit results. In the vortex field the radial bias fell from \VtxOrbThreeBiasSmall~m at $r_d = 0.01$~m to \VtxOrbThreeBiasLarge~m at 0.50~m. In the saddle field it ranged from \SadOrbThreeBiasSmall~m to \SadOrbThreeBiasLarge~m. The between-trial spread was \VtxOrbThreeBetweenMin--\VtxOrbThreeBetweenMax~m (vortex) and \SadOrbThreeBetweenMin--\SadOrbThreeBetweenMax~m (saddle), and the within-run standard deviation was \VtxOrbThreeWithinMin--\VtxOrbThreeWithinMax~m and \SadOrbThreeWithinMin--\SadOrbThreeWithinMax~m. Above $r_d = 0.05$~m the fluctuation within one orbit exceeds the variation between repeats. By the $0.25\,r_d$ criterion the practical range is $r_d \ge \VtxOrbThreeRange$~m in the vortex and $r_d \ge \SadOrbThreeRange$~m in the saddle. Below these radii the controller holds a bounded orbit about the critical point but does not track the commanded radius. Of the three-robot orbits, \VtxOrbThreeFail{} of \VtxOrbThreeRuns{} (vortex) and \SadOrbThreeFail{} of \SadOrbThreeRuns{} (saddle) failed by leaving the map, all at $r_d \ge \VtxOrbThreeFailRd$~m, where the orbit plus its within-run swing reaches the map edge.

\subsection{Error Analysis (replacement paragraphs)}

The metrics separate estimation error from control error. At $r_d = 0.01$~m the controller-only bias in the vortex was \VtxOrbThreeCtrlSmall~m against a true bias of \VtxOrbThreeBiasSmall~m, so the small-radius offset arises in the closed loop and not in the estimate, as the lag analysis above predicts. The localization error grows with distance from the critical point, from \VtxOrbThreeLocSmall~to \VtxOrbThreeLocLarge~m in the vortex and from \SadOrbThreeLocSmall~to \SadOrbThreeLocLarge~m in the saddle. This is the range-dependent amplification by $\kappa(\hat{\mathbf{J}})$, and the within-run spread grows with it.

The between-trial spread stays small while the within-run spread grows with $r_d$. An error that repeats at the same place on every lap points to the fixed distortion of the printed field. Temporally varying noise would instead change from trial to trial. Static runs with the formation held at fixed points measure the second effect directly \open{campaign static noise runs}.
